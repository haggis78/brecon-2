Welcome to the Foundation of Christ College Brecon Project

THIS SITE IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION (July 2026).

This site is a born-digital scholarly edition of a Latin document issued by King Henry VIII, the Letters Patent that founded Christ College Brecon in 1541 as part of Henry's ambition to bring the Welsh church and state fully under his control. The original Letters Patent issued under the Great Seal do not survive, but eleven copies do, eight in manuscript and three in print. By comparing these against one another on a word-for-word basis, and recording the variants in a TEI XML file, we have produced a series of reading views, established the relationships among the copies, and reconstructed the most likely textual readings of the lost original.

The project began as a professor-student collaboration at the University of Pittsburgh-Greensburg under the Center for Digital Studies. The current expansion and revision (version 2.0) was launched in 2026 by Dr. William Campbell. The project is not affiliated with Christ College Brecon itself.

For more information about the project and the history it represents, visit the About tab.
